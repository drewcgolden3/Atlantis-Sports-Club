Before You Join

The fine print, in plain English

When does billing start?

The $99 enrollment is charged today. Your $99 monthly dues don’t begin until the club opens on July 7, 2027 — you pay nothing monthly in the meantime.

What happens after the first year?

Your founding rate is held for 12 months from opening day. After that it moves to the standard $129/month for full gym + pool access. You’re never locked into a long-term contract.

Can I cancel before you open?

Yes. Cancel any time before opening day and your enrollment is refunded in full.

What does full access include?

The entire club: the fitness floor, the Olympic-size pool, the basketball court, and the group-fitness studio. There’s no separate pool fee on the founding membership.

What happens when the 500 are gone?

The founding rate closes for good. Memberships after that start at the standard $299 enrollment and $129/month.
