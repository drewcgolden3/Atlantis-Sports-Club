Before You Join

The fine print, in plain English

How often am I billed?

Every two weeks — 26 payments a year. These are not monthly rates.

When does billing start?

The $99 enrollment is charged when you join. Your biweekly dues don’t begin until the club opens on July 7, 2027 — you pay nothing every two weeks in the meantime.

Are classes included?

Group fitness classes are included in the Complete Membership only. The Fitness and Fitness + Pool memberships do not include classes.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. There’s no long-term commitment — cancel anytime. Cancel before opening day and your enrollment is refunded in full.

Can I move up a level later?

Yes. You can move from Fitness to Fitness + Pool or Complete at any time, and your Founders enrollment rate carries over.
