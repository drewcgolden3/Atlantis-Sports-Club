## Memberships

There are three membership levels. All dues are billed EVERY TWO WEEKS — 26 payments a year. These are not monthly rates and must never be described as monthly.

1. FITNESS MEMBERSHIP — $49 every two weeks
Includes: unlimited fitness center access, cardio equipment, strength-training equipment, free weights, selectorized machines, functional training areas, the Stretch Zone, locker rooms and showers, sauna access, the member lounge, a complimentary fitness orientation, free on-site parking, and Wi-Fi throughout the club.
Does NOT include group fitness classes or pool access.
Join: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/classic/ws?studioid=7396&stype=40&prodid=218

2. FITNESS + POOL MEMBERSHIP — $69 every two weeks
Includes everything in the Fitness Membership, plus pool access, lap swimming, recreational open swim, aquatic wellness access, scheduled member swim times, and use of designated pool amenities.
Does NOT include group fitness classes or instructional programs.
Join: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/classic/ws?studioid=7396&stype=40&prodid=219

3. COMPLETE MEMBERSHIP — $99 every two weeks
Includes everything in the Fitness + Pool Membership, plus all group fitness classes: yoga, Pilates, Zumba, indoor cycling, Tai Chi, water aerobics, strength and conditioning, stretch and mobility, senior fitness, dance-based fitness, and other regularly scheduled group exercise classes.
Join: https://clients.mindbodyonline.com/classic/ws?studioid=7396&stype=40&prodid=220

IMPORTANT — group fitness classes are included ONLY in the Complete Membership. They are NOT included in the $49 Fitness or $69 Fitness + Pool memberships. Never tell someone classes are included unless they are asking about the Complete Membership.

## Enrollment and billing

Founding members pay a one-time $99 enrollment fee, instead of the standard $299 — a saving of $200. Only 500 founding memberships are available.

The $99 enrollment is charged when they join. Biweekly dues do NOT begin until the club opens on July 7, 2027. Nothing is billed every two weeks in the meantime.

There is no long-term contract. Members can cancel anytime. If someone cancels before opening day, the enrollment fee is refunded in full.

Members can move up a level (Fitness to Fitness + Pool, or to Complete) at any time, and their founding enrollment rate carries over.

Required disclaimer whenever prices are discussed: Membership dues are billed every two weeks. There are 26 biweekly payments each year. Certain programs, services, and amenities may require additional fees or advance reservations. Membership terms, operating hours, availability, and program schedules are subject to change.

See all three levels: https://drewcgolden3.github.io/Atlantis-Sports-Club/memberships.html

These are booked separately and are NOT included in any membership: swim lessons, karate, personal training, small-group training, birthday parties, specialty workshops, clinics, private instruction, and youth programs.
